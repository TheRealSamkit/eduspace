Why campus study materials stay lost

Every semester, students write detailed lecture summaries and compile practice problem sets. Yet most of that work disappears into private group chats or dead shared folders.

EduSpace organizes materials by course, semester, and material type within your verified campus domain.

WhatsApp and Telegram groups

Documents get buried under hundreds of chat messages. When exam week arrives, students scroll through weeks of chat history trying to find a PDF someone sent in September.

Google Drive links and permissions

Folders set to private, links that expire when a student graduates, and confusing folder trees with no search filters or course tags.

Free-riding without contribution

A few students share everything while others only download. The credit ledger rewards active contributors while keeping downloads accessible to all verified students.

Copyright and academic integrity

Students may only upload original notes, study summaries, and materials they hold permission to distribute. Commercial textbooks, publisher question banks, and confidential exams are strictly prohibited.

Every file detail view features a report modal. Campus moderators review flagged items and issue immediate removals with credit reversals.
