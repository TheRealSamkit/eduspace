1. Ownership of Academic Materials

EduSpace is an academic peer-to-peer sharing ecosystem designed solely for students within verified university domains. Users are legally and ethically obligated to upload only materials they own or have the explicit right to share (such as original student notes, student study summaries, and personal worked solutions).

Strict Prohibition: Do not upload copyrighted textbooks, unreleased exam papers, commercial test bank questions, or faculty slides without institutional copyright clearance.

2. Institutional Isolation & Privacy

Materials uploaded to EduSpace are strictly isolated to verified students who hold an email address from the corresponding institutional domain. Students from other institutions can never browse, search, or download your materials.

3. Reporting Infringements & Inappropriate Content

Every study material card includes a Report button (). If you believe a shared document infringes copyright or breaches academic integrity, submit a report directly from the document's detail page.

4. Administrator Takedown Policy & Credit Reversal

University administrators inspect reported materials. Upon confirming an infringement:

  • The offending material is immediately removed from the repository.
  • Any upload reward credits earned by the uploader are fully reversed in the credit ledger.
  • Repeated infractions may result in account termination under institutional honor codes.

5. How Credits Work

EduSpace operates an academic credit economy designed to encourage fair peer-to-peer exchange:

  • Starting balance: New student accounts receive {{ config.SIGNUP_BONUS }} credits upon registration.
  • Uploading: Verified uploads award {{ config.UPLOAD_REWARD }} credits (capped at {{ config.MAX_REWARDED_UPLOADS_PER_DAY }} rewarded uploads daily).
  • Downloading: Downloading peer study material costs {{ config.DOWNLOAD_COST }} credits. Re-downloading previously purchased documents or your own uploads is always free.
  • Balance protection: Downloads require an adequate credit balance. Credits never go below zero.
